# Amathuba: Opportunity Starts Here.

This charter is the foundation of Amathuba. Every feature, partnership and decision should be checked against it.

## Mission
Empower unemployed South Africans through accessible career resources, tools and opportunities.

## Vision
Make employment support accessible regardless of financial circumstances.

## Core principle
Essential job-seeker services remain free.

Building a CV, finding opportunities, applying (including the Z83), tracking applications, preparing for interviews and staying safe from scams are essential services. Job seekers will never pay for them. Any income must come from sponsors, grants, donations, employers or optional in-person services, never from people looking for work.

## Our promise
Amathuba serves people with no work experience just as fully as experienced professionals: from someone looking for their first opportunity to someone ready for their next career move.

That means every feature must work for both:
- a school leaver whose experience is school, volunteering, church, sport, caring for family or informal work, and
- a professional with years of experience and qualifications.

## Long-term ambition
Grow into a South African employment-support ecosystem.

## How we decide
Before building or changing anything, ask:
1. Does it help someone find, prepare for, or get work?
2. Does it keep essential services free for job seekers?
3. Does it work for someone with no experience as well as someone with ten years?
4. Does it work on a basic phone with little data?
5. Does it protect people's privacy and safety?

If the answer to any of these is no, rethink it.

## Design commitments
- **Free and open to everyone:** no sign-up and no subscription for essential services.
- **Light on data:** works on basic phones and keeps working offline after the first visit.
- **Private by default:** CVs and personal details stay on the user's device. Visits are counted anonymously, without cookies.
- **Safe:** opportunities are checked before posting, and we never post anything that asks job seekers for money.
- **Plain language:** clear words that people with any level of education can follow.

## Where it began
Amathuba means "opportunities" in isiXhosa and isiZulu. It was created by Onele Nongindzi, inspired by unemployed young people in Lusikisiki, Eastern Cape, and it is built for every South African looking for work.
