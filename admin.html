Post a local opportunity

For Onele Foundation volunteers. Fill in the form, copy the updated list, and paste it into GitHub. The opportunity then appears in Amathuba under “Opportunities posted by Onele Foundation”, and job seekers can filter by province.

Only post opportunities you have checked with the employer, municipality or official notice. Never post anything that asks applicants for money.
