# Amathuba 1.0 release notes

**Opportunity Starts Here.** Free employment support for South African job seekers, from a first opportunity to the next career move.

## What Amathuba does
- **Your CV:** build an ATS-friendly CV, or upload a PDF or Word CV to have it polished: spelling fixed, standard headings, private details removed. People with no work experience get ready-made examples for volunteering, school, church, caring for family, small businesses and sport.
- **Where to apply:** job titles and opportunities that match your qualification, live listings on Pnet, Indeed, Graduates24 and LinkedIn, and checked opportunities posted by Onele Foundation, filtered by province or your location.
- **My applications:** track every application, get calendar reminders, keep a documents checklist.
- **Z83 helper:** fills in the official Z83 for government jobs, including your ID number in its boxes, and joins it with your CV as one PDF.
- **Interview practice** and a **scam checker** for job offers.
- **Sharing:** WhatsApp sharing, “Send my CV” and a printable QR poster.

## New in this release
- Examples for first-time job seekers, and a CV check that catches leftover example text.
- “Did Amathuba help you today?” and anonymous counts of interviews and offers, so we can see the difference Amathuba makes.
- “Clear all my data from this phone”, for shared phones and computers.
- Safer handling of links in tracked applications.

## Privacy
CVs and personal details stay on the user's phone. Visits are counted anonymously, without cookies.
