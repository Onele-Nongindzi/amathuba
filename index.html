    Z83 helper for government jobs

    Every government post needs the Z83 application form. Many applications are thrown out because the form is old, incomplete or unsigned. Fill in your answers here, then copy them neatly onto the official form.

    Use only the new Z83, marked “Effective 01 January 2021” at the bottom of the first page. The old form is disqualified. Download it free from dpsa.gov.za, and never pay anyone for it.
    Part A: The post you’re applying for

    Use a new form for every post. Copy these exactly from the advert.

    Part B: Personal information

    Your details must match your ID or passport exactly.

    Answer every yes/no question honestly. A false answer can lead to disqualification or dismissal later. If you answer yes, the form asks for details: give them on the form.

    Part C: Contact details
    Part D: Official language proficiency

    For each South African language you know, say whether you speak it and read or write it “good”, “fair” or “poor”.

    Part E: Formal qualifications

    From highest to lowest: school or institution, qualification and year obtained. Add any current studies too.

    Parts F and G: Work experience and references

    Many departments accept “Refer to attached CV” for work experience and references, because your CV has the details. Check the advert. One question in Part F must always be answered:

    More for the official form

    These go straight onto the official Z83 when you tap “Fill the official Z83” below.

    Part F: your jobs, with reasons for leaving

    Taken from your CV (up to three jobs). Change the jobs in step 1.

    Part G: references

    Signature and initials (optional)

    Draw with your finger, or type your initials. Drawn initials are used if you draw them. Some departments accept a signature drawn on a phone; others want a handwritten signature on a printed copy, so check the advert. If you skip this, print the filled form and sign and initial it by hand.

    Before you send it

      The answer sheet is a guide for you to copy from. It is not the official form and can’t be submitted in its place. Always read the advert too: some departments add their own rules.

      Interview practice

      Practise answers to the questions panels ask most. Type your answer, read the tip, and say it out loud a few times. Your answers are saved on this device only.

      Use STAR for “tell us about a time…” questions

      Situation: where and when. Task: what you had to do. Action: the steps you took. Result: what happened, with a number if you can.

      Interview day checklist

        Scam checker

        Got a job offer by WhatsApp, SMS, email or Facebook? Paste it here and Amathuba will look for the warning signs of a job scam. Nothing you paste leaves this phone.

        Rules that keep you safe

        Real employers and government never ask you to pay for a job, training, uniforms or “admin”. Never send your ID, bank details, PIN or a one-time PIN (OTP) before you have checked the job is real. Look the company up yourself and call its official number, not the number in the message. Government jobs are advertised on gov.za websites and in the Public Service Vacancy Circular.
