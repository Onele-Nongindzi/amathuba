CV check

    Match a job advert (optional)

    Paste the advert. You’ll see which important words your CV already has and which are missing. Add missing ones only if they’re true for you.

    Cover letter

    A short letter that goes with your CV. Fill in the job, choose “Write my letter”, then edit it so it sounds like you. If you pasted a job advert above, the letter uses the matching skills.

    Keep it to one page. For government posts, the Z83 and CV are usually all you need, so only add a letter if the advert asks for one.

    Where to apply

    Choose your highest education, field and age in step 1 to see what fits you.

    Stay safe: real employers and government programmes never ask you to pay to apply, to get a job, or for “training fees” before you start. Use the official websites below and report anyone who asks for money.

    Links and age rules change often. Always check the details on the official website before applying.

    My applications

    Keep track of every job you apply for, so you know what’s closing soon and when to follow up. Saved on this device only.

    Add an application

    Z83 helper for government jobs

    Every government post needs the Z83 application form. Many applications are thrown out because the form is old, incomplete or unsigned. Fill in your answers here, then copy them neatly onto the official form.

    Use only the new Z83, marked “Effective 01 January 2021” at the bottom of the first page. The old form is disqualified. Download it free from dpsa.gov.za, and never pay anyone for it.
    Part A: The post you’re applying for

    Use a new form for every post. Copy these exactly from the advert.

    Part B: Personal details

    Answer every yes/no question honestly. A false answer can lead to dismissal later. Never leave one blank.

    Part C: Contact details
    Part D: Qualifications

    Before you send it

      The answer sheet is a guide for you to copy from. It is not the official form and can’t be submitted in its place. Always read the advert too: some departments add their own rules.

      Interview practice

      Practise answers to the questions panels ask most. Type your answer, read the tip, and say it out loud a few times. Your answers are saved on this device only.

      Use STAR for “tell us about a time…” questions

      Situation: where and when. Task: what you had to do. Action: the steps you took. Result: what happened, with a number if you can.
